## CI_DEVICE_BOUNDARY (elevated to a core architecture document)

- **Layer 1 — Linux CI (exists, `ci.yml`):** pure Swift — Domain + LyricEngine (`swift:6.0` container). Cannot ever host MelodyKit (AVFoundation).
- **Layer 2 — macOS/Xcode CI (Q10):** MelodyKit WAV-fixture DSP via the verified `analyzeFile` path; app-target compilation; SpeechAnalyzer offline harness (Q3); FoundationModels benchmarks on an Apple-Intelligence Mac (Q4/Q7).
- **Layer 3 — physical device:** mic capture, routes/Bluetooth/USB, interruptions (Q2), real buffer timing, on-device Apple Intelligence latency/thermal/battery, perceived latency. **Constraint:** dev iPhone 13 is Apple-Intelligence-ineligible — on-device FM items need TestFlight or borrowed A18-class hardware.
- **Layer 4 — human musical evaluation:** perception sweep (Q6), candidate quality (Q4/Q7/Q9), phrase-boundary quality, flow-mode trust, naturalness.
