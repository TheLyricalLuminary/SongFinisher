# App Store listing & featuring plan

Working copy for App Store Connect metadata. The rule that governs every line here:
**never promise the AI experience to hardware that will get the offline assembler.**
Apple Intelligence (on-device Foundation Models) requires roughly iPhone 15 Pro or
later; everything below is written so an iPhone 13 owner reading the listing is not
misled — that's where 1-star reviews and review flags come from.

## Name & subtitle

- **Name:** Song Finisher
- **Subtitle:** Sing a melody. Keep the words.

## Description (draft)

Hum, sing, or play a phrase — Song Finisher listens to its rhythm, mood, and harmony,
and suggests lyric lines that actually fit it: right syllable count, right stresses,
right feel.

Everything happens on your device. Your melody is analyzed locally in real time —
nothing is uploaded, no account, no cloud. Audio is never stored; only the lyrics you
choose to keep.

- Real music analysis: pitch, tempo, phrasing, and chords, detected live
- Lines sized to your melody: syllables and stresses matched, not approximated
- On supported devices, Apple Intelligence writes original lines on-device*
- Keep what works: accepted lines build a song sheet you can export and share
- Keep singing: sessions continue on the lock screen with a Live Activity
- Start hands-free: "Start a session" works from Siri and Shortcuts

*On-device AI lyric writing requires a device that supports Apple Intelligence
(iPhone 15 Pro or later). On other devices, Song Finisher offers instant offline
draft suggestions built from its bundled songwriting lexicon — clearly labeled in
the app, so you always know which you're getting.

## Keywords (draft)

songwriting, lyrics, melody, songwriter, music, writing, hum, compose, verse, rhyme

## Privacy nutrition label

- Data collected: **none**. (Matches `PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy`: no tracking, no
  collected data types.)

## Featuring nomination (App Store Connect → Featuring Nominations)

Submit 6–8 weeks before launch. Hooks, in pitch order:

1. **On-device Foundation Models adoption** — a creative tool where Apple
   Intelligence writes lyric lines constrained by live DSP analysis of the user's own
   melody. Not a chatbot wrapper: the model receives syllable/stress/tempo/chord
   specs derived on-device and every line is re-verified against them on-device.
2. **Private by architecture** — mic audio never leaves the device, never touches a
   server, is never stored. The privacy story isn't a policy, it's the design.
3. **Founder story** — journeyman welder and signed songwriter, built solo. Pitch to
   Apple-adjacent press (MacStories loves indie + new-framework apps) in the same
   window as the nomination.

## Editorial demo path (rehearse before any submission)

Install → first-run onboarding (3 screens) → mic permission → sing one clear phrase
(~4–8 notes, a beat of silence to close the phrase) → lines appear, badged
ON-DEVICE AI → tap Use → line lands in the song sheet. Practice in office-level
ambient noise on the actual demo device. If any step stumbles, fix that before
polishing anything else.

## App preview video (15s, also the TikTok cut)

Phone on a table, one continuous take: hum → watch the syllable dots fill → card
flips up with a line that fits → tap Use → lock the phone → Live Activity on the
lock screen. No captions needed; the loop is the pitch.
