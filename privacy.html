Privacy Policy

Last updated 29 July 2026

Song Finisher does not collect, transmit, or store your personal information anywhere outside your own device. There is no account, no sign-up, and no server. This isn't a policy choice that could quietly change — the app has no networking code in it.

Your audio

When you start a session, Song Finisher listens through your microphone to detect the pitch, rhythm, and phrasing of what you sing or play. That audio is analyzed in real time, in memory, on your device. It is never written to disk, never uploaded, and never shared. When the session ends, it is gone.

If you choose to open an existing audio file for analysis, it is read locally and treated the same way.

Your lyrics and songs

Lyric suggestions are generated entirely on your device — either by Apple's on-device Foundation Models framework (on Apple Intelligence-capable hardware) or by a dictionary and rule set bundled inside the app. Neither path contacts a server, and the app works in airplane mode.

Songs and lyric lines you keep are saved locally on your device. They are not backed up to us, because there is no "us" to back up to. Deleting the app deletes them.

What we don't do

  • No analytics or usage tracking
  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers
  • No third-party SDKs collecting data
  • No cross-app or cross-site tracking of any kind
  • No selling or sharing of data — there is no data to sell

Purchases

If you buy Song Finisher Pro, the purchase is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. Apple processes your payment and tells the app whether a valid purchase exists. We never see your name, payment details, or Apple ID. Apple's handling of that transaction is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Microphone — required, so the app can hear the melody you're working on. Without it, the app cannot function. You can revoke it at any time in your device's Settings.

Children

Song Finisher is not directed at children under 13 and collects no information from anyone, regardless of age.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date. If a future version of the app were ever to send data off the device, that change would be described here plainly and before it shipped.
