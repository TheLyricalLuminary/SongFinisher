Help with Song Finisher

Song Finisher is made by one person. If something's broken or confusing, email me directly and you'll get a real answer: thelyricalluminary@gmail.com

What the app does

Play or sing a musical phrase. Song Finisher listens, works out where the phrase ended and what its rhythm and stresses are, and suggests lyric lines built to fit that exact phrase — the right syllable count, stresses landing where the strong beats are, and a mood drawn from how you played it.

Requirements

  • iPhone running iOS 17 or later, or a Mac running macOS 14 or later.
  • Microphone access.
  • The on-device AI writing is available on Apple Intelligence-capable hardware (iPhone 15 Pro and later, and Apple silicon Macs). On other devices the app still works and still suggests lines — see below.

Why do some suggestions say "OFFLINE DRAFT"?

The app has two writing engines. On Apple Intelligence hardware it uses Apple's on-device model, and those cards are labelled ON-DEVICE AI. Everywhere else — and once a free user has used their daily AI suggestions — it falls back to a built-in engine that assembles lines from a bundled dictionary. Those cards are labelled OFFLINE DRAFT.

The label refers to which engine wrote the line, not to your internet connection. Song Finisher never uses the internet at all — both engines run on your device, and the whole app works in airplane mode.

The app isn't hearing me

  • Check that microphone access is allowed: Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone on iPhone, or System Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone on Mac.
  • On a Mac, make sure the right input device is selected in System Settings → Sound → Input. If you're using an audio interface, pick it there.
  • Watch the coloured dot next to the tempo. Green means a strong, clear signal; red means the app is struggling to hear a pitch. Move closer, play a little louder, or reduce background noise.

No suggestions are appearing

The app waits for a complete musical phrase — it needs a short gap of quiet to know you've finished one. If you play continuously without pausing, it will keep waiting. Try playing a phrase, then resting for a beat.

What is Flow mode?

Normally you tap Use to keep a line. With Flow mode on, playing your next phrase automatically keeps the line that was on screen — so you never take your hands off the instrument. Kept lines appear at the bottom of the screen, and Undo keep takes back the most recent one.

Keyboard and pedal shortcuts

With a keyboard connected: space keeps a line, ← and → browse alternatives, M asks for more like the current one, R regenerates, U undoes the last keep. Bluetooth page-turner pedals send these same keystrokes, so you can drive the app with your foot while playing.

Purchases and restoring

Song Finisher Pro removes the daily limit on AI-written lines. If you've paid but the app doesn't recognise it — for example on a new device — open the upgrade screen and tap Restore Purchases. Subscriptions are managed in your Apple ID settings, not in the app.
