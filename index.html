Song Finisher

Play a line. Finish the song.

Song Finisher listens while you sing or play, hears when you land a complete musical phrase, and suggests lyric lines built to fit it — the right number of syllables, stresses on the strong beats, in the mood you played it.

Everything happens on your device. No account, no cloud, no internet required.
